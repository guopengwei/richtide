## Question

Which plausible first Commercial Markets can support the approved `zh-CN` RichTide offer, considering eligible customers, A-share/Hong Kong/US instrument research, market-data rights, Airwallex capabilities, currency and tax handling, refunds and consumer rights, privacy, and financial-promotion or adviser boundaries?

## Resolution evidence

- Primary-source comparison of viable candidate markets.
- Explicit unknowns and facts requiring legal, tax, data-provider, or payment-provider confirmation.
- A recommended candidate set and the trade-offs the human launch-envelope decision must resolve.

## Resolution comment

Primary-source research narrowed the first-market decision to Hong Kong and Singapore. Prefer Hong Kong conditionally for audience, currency, payment-rail, and tax fit; retain Singapore as fallback or second. Neither is launch-approved. Selection requires written regulatory-perimeter advice, Airwallex merchant and capability approval, field-level A/H/US data-rights contracts, tax and privacy approval, and consumer-terms review. Australia, the United Kingdom, the United States, and Mainland China remain outside the initial paid launch envelope pending separate qualification.
