## Question

Which instrument classes and exchanges are supported initially, and which source tiers authoritatively provide identity, quotes, OHLCV, corporate actions, statements, filings, events, calendars, enrichment, retention, and redistribution?

## Resolution evidence

- Canonical instrument identity and supported-universe rules.
- Field-level source authority, fallback, freshness, and outage behavior.
- Point-in-time, licensing, attribution, retention, and derived-data boundaries.
- Explicit research-only sources excluded from paid production.
