## Question

Which responsive information architecture and task flow lets a self-directed retail investor find an instrument, understand freshness and free evidence, choose an eligible analysis, monitor changes, and return to prior research without turning RichTide into a chatbot or trading terminal?

## Resolution evidence

- Desktop and mobile navigation prototype.
- Public, authenticated, empty, loading, error, stale, locked, and returning-user paths.
- Tested six-tab instrument-page structure and cross-product navigation.
- Human reaction and resulting decision.
