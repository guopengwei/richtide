## Question

How should a Customer register, verify identity, compare plans, subscribe, buy a prepaid usage pack, return from hosted checkout, inspect distinct balances and activity, change plan, cancel, understand retained credits, and request an eligible refund in clear Simplified Chinese?

## Resolution evidence

- Responsive customer commercial-journey prototype.
- Pending verification, payment failure, restricted account, tax, expiry, downgrade, cancellation, refund, dispute, and recovery states.
- Accessibility, consent, disclosure, and no-surprise-charge checks.
- Human reaction and resulting decision.
