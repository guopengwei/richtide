## Question

How should the shared privileged shell separate case-scoped support, catalog and reconciliation administration, and source/job/quality operations while making authority, stale projections, reasons, approvals, audit, and destructive consequences unmistakable?

## Resolution evidence

- Responsive prototypes for all three privileged domains.
- Purpose-bound access, approval, reconciliation, quality-block, incident, and break-glass scenarios.
- Clear separation of authoritative state from projections.
- Human reaction and resulting decision.
