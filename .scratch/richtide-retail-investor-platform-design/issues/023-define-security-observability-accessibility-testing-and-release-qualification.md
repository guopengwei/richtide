## Question

What measurable security, privacy, provenance, quality, observability, SLO, accessibility, responsive, localization, browser, disaster-recovery, commercial, and human-approval evidence is required for each delivery phase and final production-readiness claim?

## Resolution evidence

- Requirement-to-evidence acceptance matrix.
- Threat, abuse, entitlement-leakage, ledger-invariant, gateway, source, method, browser, accessibility, and recovery tests.
- SLOs, alerts, dashboards, runbooks, ownership, and rollback gates.
- Explicit separation of local proof, staging proof, commercial approval, and production launch authority.
