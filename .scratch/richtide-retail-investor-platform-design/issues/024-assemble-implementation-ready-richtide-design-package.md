## Question

Assemble the resolved product, domain, UX, visual, analytical, commercial-control, architecture, security, operations, acceptance, and prototype decisions into one coherent implementation-ready RichTide design package without duplicating or weakening the authoritative ticket resolutions.

## Resolution evidence

- Consolidated design specification and domain glossary.
- Linked high-fidelity and interactive prototype assets.
- Architecture, state, authority, data-flow, and release-gate diagrams.
- Traceability from every map decision to its final design home.
- Handoff checklist with unresolved external approvals clearly separated from implementation decisions.
